## Four Beats

1. **Name the process.** Give the QForm UK process model a short title before you guess fill from a sketch.
2. **Pin the live jobs.** Keep current work on QForm UK metal forming and leave unused dies aside.
3. **Read the forge.** Open QForm UK forging simulation before you move a die that already has fill.
4. **Check the fill.** Read QForm UK die fill before you archive a process.
5. **Keep the pair.** Leave forming and simulation on the same QForm UK process model.
6. **Leave the press ready.** Keep die fill aligned with QForm UK metal forming.
7. **Close the loop.** Clear finished processes so the next QForm UK visit does not start with a search.

## First Questions

**Does QForm UK replace every other program?**
No. QForm UK sits beside the press list, browser, and files you already use.

**Can two people share one computer?**
Yes. Each person keeps a separate QForm UK profile.

**What should a new user try first?**
One small QForm UK metal forming job today, then the same die fill tomorrow.

**Does die fill leave the process model?**
No. QForm UK die fill stays with QForm UK forging simulation on that model.

**Is QForm UK only for a finished press run?**
No. QForm UK can hold a single die while the rest of the forge stays off the board.

**Does the press need a second file for fill?**
No. QForm UK keeps forming and die fill on this process model.

**Will a forge review stay readable?**
Yes. A named QForm UK process model keeps fill visible before the press starts.

## Field Note

QForm UK is easier to trust when the process model stays smaller than the folder it came from. A named process shows what is active, the forming pins show what you will review today, and the closing note tells the next person which die fill changed.

The same loop fits a single forge, a short fill check, or a desk shared by two people. Open the last QForm UK process model, finish one die fill, and leave fewer guessed sketches than you found.

A review should not have to guess which die belongs to the open fill. Keep that pair together inside QForm UK.

## One Real Pass

Pick a process that already has a name. Open it in QForm UK, keep only that job on the process model, and finish one die fill you can see.

If the pass feels crowded, the set is too wide. Drop anything that belongs to a different press and leave it out of this QForm UK process model.
